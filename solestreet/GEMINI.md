# UI/UX & Design Guidelines

## Stitch MCP Integration for UI/UX
- When the user asks to design, prototype, mockup, or generate new UI/UX screens or page layouts, automatically use the **Stitch MCP tools** (`ServerName: "stitch"`, such as `generate_screen_from_text`, `edit_screens`, `create_project`, `create_design_system`, `generate_variants`) to design the visual concepts and screens.
- Leverage Stitch's design system capabilities to maintain brand consistency for SoleStreet.
- After creating or iterating on the screens in Stitch, seamlessly implement and translate those approved UI/UX designs into the Next.js / Tailwind codebase.
